RYUK® DESIGN STUDIO ✦ 4K BRAND IDENTITY SPEC

Logo & Visual Identity Exploration

Four distinct conceptual logo systems engineered for 4K video mastering, high-contrast dark environments, and responsive digital products. Each concept explores geometric precision, chromatic velocity, and cinematic presence inspired by studio reference archetypes.

← Back to Main Studio Site
CONCEPT 01

The Kinetic R

Ref: Circular Velocity

The Kinetic R translates the bold circular trajectory of reference 01 into an aerodynamic studio monogram. Built with high-speed chamfers and a forward-lunging diagonal strut that communicates immediate launch velocity and directional focus.

GEOMETRY: 48PX MONOLITH STROKE
CONCEPT 02

The Modular Slab R

Ref: Velotile Modular Blocks

The Modular Slab R is inspired directly by the Velotile reference geometry: pure architectural components separated by precision hairline voids. The neon-pink detached stepping strut creates a high-tension focal point engineered for modern tech brands.

GEOMETRY: MODULAR GRID 16PX GAP
CONCEPT 03

The Liquid Chrome R

Ref: 3D Iridescent Chrome

The Liquid Chrome R captures the tactile luxury of reference 03: a heavy 3D beveled block sculpted with liquid mercury reflections, hot-pink caustic flares, and sunset amber rim lighting. Optimized for cinema bumper animations and splash screens.

SPECULAR: DUAL-STOP CAUSTIC MAP
CONCEPT 04

The Cinema Shutter R

Studio Emblem: Optical Prism

The Cinema Shutter R is an optical mark that combines a camera lens aperture blade with an anamorphic ratio prism. Crafted with precision hairline strokes and a glowing focal core to highlight Ryuk’s craft as a specialized launch film director.

OPTICS: 12PX VECTOR CONTOUR
