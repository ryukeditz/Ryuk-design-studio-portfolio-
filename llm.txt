## What We Make

- **Launch Films**: 30–60s flagship motion films for major product releases, funding rounds, and hero marketing campaigns.
- **Product Videos**: 60–90s feature choreography and end-to-end interface walkthroughs showcasing real user workflows in action.
- **Explainers**: 60–90s problem-to-solution narrative films breaking down complex technical architectures into clear mental models.
- **Social / Campaign Cutdowns**: 6–15s modular hooks, feature highlights, and performance ad cuts tailored for LinkedIn, X, and YouTube.

## Engagement Model & Turnaround

- **Turnaround Time**: Standard project timelines range between **3 to 5 weeks** from initial kickoff to final mastered delivery.
- **Scoping & Budget**: Fixed-scope project pricing tailored to narrative scope, 3D fidelity, interface complexity, and deliverable packages.
- **Scope Included**: Narrative architecture, scriptwriting, storyboarding, art direction, 3D/UI motion design, custom sound design, revisions, and multi-format exports.
